## Imprint caveat

Whatever imprint you enter should match the publisher name registered against that
ISBN at Bowker (myidentifiers.com), if you bought the ISBN yourself. Your ISBNs come
from at least three prefixes (`979-8-2409`, `979-8-2561`, `979-8-1827`), which
suggests more than one source — worth confirming before entering a name that
contradicts the registration. If Ingram assigned the ISBN, this does not apply.

IngramSpark supports multiple imprints on one account (account settings), which is how
you keep Post Peleos and Søren Stromberg separate without a second account.
