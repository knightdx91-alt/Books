## Strongest Disruption
Cutting **"Never before in my hearing had he said it to a stranger…"** and letting the dog's nose take the beat. "My wife," said to a porter with a tablet, is the payoff of Ch.15's bargain. The narrator's gloss told the reader what to feel and why. Without it, the chained dog pushes into Mara's hand, the porter moves them on, and the very next sentence is Plautia on the stair with her face turned away. The jealousy, the marriage and the rival all sit in three lines with nothing explained. Close second: the bare **"When his letter came, did she weep?"**. With the look at the sheet gone, Plautia's question arrives like the slap her card describes.
