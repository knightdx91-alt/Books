## Opening (Hook)

**Original (77 words):**
> Rome reached me first through the curtains of a hired chair, as noise and smell: hammers all round us, a stonecutter's mallet
> going steadily somewhere overhead, a man on a scaffold bawling down for his bucket and being answered from three streets off, a
> peddler crying that he would take broken glass for sulfur matches, a dog that kept pace with the bearers half the length of a
> street giving its opinion of them, and under it all, whenever the poles swung riverward, the Tiber, which smelled of black mud,
> old drains and wet rope.

**New (98 words):**
> Rome reached me first through the curtains of a hired chair, as noise: a stonecutter's mallet going somewhere overhead until a
> man on a scaffold drowned it, bawling down for his bucket, and was drowned in his turn by a peddler crying that he would take
> broken glass for sulfur matches, and the peddler by a dog that kept pace with the bearers half the length of a street giving its
> opinion of them, and all of it, whenever the poles swung riverward, by the smell of the Tiber, which was black mud, old drains
> and wet rope.

- **Type: SENSORY IMMERSION (the long spiral).** Not IN MEDIAS RES ✓. Nothing is under way except arriving, and the reader is
  placed by the senses, not by inference.
- **Why rewritten:** the brief asked for a true spiral, not a list. The original was a catalogue after a colon: five noises set
  side by side, with "hammers all round us" and the "mallet" saying the same thing twice. The rewrite keeps every image except the
  doubled hammers and "answered from three streets off". It turns the catalogue into a chain where each thing is drowned out by
  the next. Each new noise is nearer the chair than the last: overhead, then the scaffold, then the street, then the dog at the
  bearers' heels. After that a different sense, the river smell, drowns the lot. The sentence circles inward to the box she is
  sitting in, and that makes it a spiral. It still ends on the concrete Tiber triad.
- **Connection:** the first word is still "Rome". That answers Ch.21's open question (Rome or no) without staging a choice, as the
  progress.md brief requires.
- **Score: 8/10** (original 7). Information gap 2/3: Rome, so he came, and why a chair (the next paragraph answers it with the
  belly). Emotional pull 2/3: comic momentum, a voice enjoying the city, and the river stink under it. Voice 2/2: the peddler's
  broken glass and the dog's "opinion" are Mara's dry eye. Connection 2/2.
- **Alternatives considered (not applied):**
  - CONTRAST: open on the R-volume tissue overlay against the living street (est. 7). It puts 2026 first and wastes the ledger's
    sensory spiral.
  - QUESTION: open on Plautia's built hair ("a frame inside") (est. 6). This breaks `long-spiral` and puts Rome second.
