## Changes Made

### Subtext (outline layer kept: they argue the contract; the son asks whether the father is ashamed, and nobody says it)
- **Felix, l.~224:** "Not once in more than two summers. Not even to look." became "Two summers and more. Not even to look. Why's that?" The surface topic is why Valens hasn't come to the sand. Underneath is the shame question. Technique: **deflection.** Valens answers a different question ("Albucius wants me kept off his gate. He says they watch who comes asking."). The answer is true and practical, and it is not what Felix asked.
- **Felix, "Robbed me?"** replaces "Never." It throws Valens's word back at him as a question, so the argument stays on money.
- **Felix, Rusticus:** "and he only fights to buy the next field along" (was "Nicanor says he fights so he can…"). Felix now owns the scorn, and it lines up with the closing line's fame-versus-field contrast.
- **Tunnel:** "You saw him?" opens Felix's rush. He assumes Valens was watching. The last line then tells us he looked up and found him sitting. The closing line is untouched.
- Valens's "Mine." and "That wants washing." are untouched. No one names the shame, and there are no speeches about honor or the arena.

### Voice Adjustments
- **Valens:** the mind-reading speech is cut to his own register. The pricing lecture ("The giver pays more for a corpse than for one that walks off. Albucius knows which pays him better.") is out. In its place is "You heard him. The duumvir pays him for the dead." He turns Albucius's own words against him, he hears greed, and there's no comparison in his mouth. "Albucius says you'll fight better if…" had a comparison in his speech; it is now the plain report above. "Then it's Lupus's boy on the sand" became "…and it's Lupus's boy on the sand" (cuts a "Then" opener). "Not once, walking out to my mark" became "Walking out to my mark." "I said it at Kirrha with the Cilician listening, there on the quay" became "I said it at Kirrha, with the Cilician listening."
- **Felix:** "with Nicanor by to hear me say it all through, and I never stumbled once" became "with Nicanor listening, and I never tripped on a word of it." "made on my own legs" became "The smith measured me for them," so "They're made to my legs" in his next speech doesn't double. "Nicanor told me all that my first day" became "I had all that my first day." "Nicanor would let you" became "They'd let you in."
- **Albucius:** "Sit, the pair of you" became "Sit. There's the bench." ("the pair of you" was Grata's earlier in the chapter.) "I'd have wagered on today" became "I'd have bet on today." "My trainer wrote the name in" became "Nicanor wrote the name in" (it had doubled "my trainer"). "Bring this again. A tiro who's been out once and lived isn't worth what one is who hasn't…" became "Come back once the games are done, and bring your cloth. A tiro who's been out once and lived, the price comes down on him, and I'll sell him back cheaper than what's in it." That's trader's idiom and cuts one comparison.
- **Nicanor:** "the lad" ×3 became ×1 ("off him", "sell him back after, cheaper than today").
- **Prima:** the stiff oath relay ("recited all of it…; he'd repeated it to the magistrate's man word for word with their trainer standing by. Twice over he told it me") became "said me the lot. The burning and the chains and the rods and dying by the iron, whatever they like to do to him. Same words he gave the magistrate's man. Twice, so I'd have it right." Mara's two prompts were stiff ("What were his words?" / "What was your answer?"). The first is now "Saying what?" The second is cut, so Prima runs straight on into "I called him an idiot." That fits her card: short answers, then one run. "the baker's nephew, the Nola Gate bakery" became "from the Nola Gate bakery". "he lifts his shield higher than anyone in the yard" became "he holds his shield up all day".
- **Grata:** "my boy" ×3 became ×2.
- **Mara:** "A hunt. The wall gives no animals." became "A hunt. Nobody wrote what kind."
