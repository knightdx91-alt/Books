## Changes Made

### Voice and law fixes (5)
1. **Grata, S2:** "My man says it was nothing. He never woke for it." → "The man I married says it was nothing, and he never woke for it."
   - Avoids her 3rd "My man" and a 4th "my husband". The joke is kept: he never woke, yet he has a view on it. The narration's "starting her husband on the price of lime" is untouched.
2. **Grata, S6:** "You've run yourself scarlet, Greek, you'll drop in my pot." → "You've run yourself scarlet, and that girl's wanted the breast this hour past."
   - Drops the 2nd "Greek" address and the doubled "my pot".
   - She is still telling Mara about herself, and she reads her own earlier observation ("the girl chewing my sleeve") as hunger.
   - It quietly pays off the run's "Aelia had last fed at dawn … my breasts, which were full", and it shows a mother of three noticing, without a question.
   - "They're yours again, my pot's catching." stays as the flood's closing excuse.
3. **Plasterer:** "Tell her she's after the cooper and the widow and this fellow here. Tomorrow. I'll not get to her today." → "Tell her she's behind the cooper and the widow and this fellow here. Tomorrow, if the lime comes."
   - Removes Grata's "-'ll not" and the doubled time phrase.
   - The new tail ties to Grata's lime grumble from his side of the trade. It is the same subject in fresh words.
4. **Cookshop man:** "Nothing to run from," he said. "Said so, didn't I." → "The small shake did that. I'd just told you it was over." (untagged; "grinning" in the paragraph before identifies him).
   - The situation still comes back on him (the aftershock Mara felt at the door split his jar), but the line is fresh.
   - The "Said so" family also had 20+ uses book-wide.
5. **Old men, S3 (added, 22 words):** "It was the year my mule died," one said. / "You were riding that mule to Nola all the spring after," said the other.
   - The brief names their argument as a voice to check, but in the draft it was only reported. Two lines now give it a life: a year dated by a mule, and an old grudge that catches the other man out.
   - The narrated payoff later in S6 is unchanged: they "had settled the year between them and gone on to roof tiles".
   - The lines contain no portent and do not use "the year of" (Grata's in Ch.28).
