## The book's own laws

- **NO FORESHADOWING (target 0): PASS, 0.** Gate 0, and read by hand for families A–E. Every narration "would" is habitual, purposive or counterfactual ("where a hoof would come down", "so that it would stand", "Valens would have gone by").
