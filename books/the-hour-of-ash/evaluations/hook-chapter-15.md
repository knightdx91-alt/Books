## Opening (Hook)

> Bells came ahead of the light, the herdsman's ewes moving about the hollow's farther end in the dark and cropping as they
> went, so that their bells sounded in no order at all, one, then three together, then a long space of nothing; behind the
> bells came the cold, […] and behind the cold came the thyme, crushed under my own feet […] The summit took the sun first
> and held it, gold, while everything under it was still blue.

- **Type: SENSORY IMMERSION (the long spiral).** This matches the writer's typing. Bells, then cold, then thyme, then dew,
  each arriving "behind" the one before it, in the single long sentence the outline asks for. The second sentence then
  turns the scene to colour (gold summit, blue below).
- **Why it is not VOICE (barred).** The hook works through sense data placed in order, not through the narrator's
  personality. Her voice is present (the counted bells, "however hot the day before had been"), but it does not carry the
  hook. Nothing in sentences 1–5 is a remark or a confession, and nothing gets its interest from attitude.
- **Not a weather or Snoopy opening.** The dawn is required by the ledger and the outline ("bells, dew, thyme, cold").
  A person enters in sentence 1 ("my own feet"). By sentence 5 there is action with a small worry in it: Valens lifting the
  black's near fore, then "Is it hot again?"
- **Score: 7/10.**
  - Information gap 1/3. The gap comes from dislocation rather than from an odd detail. Ch.14 left her at the foot of the
    cave path at dawn. Ch.15 puts her above the plateau, months later, beside a herdsman nobody has met. The reader's open
    question, what the cave did, is held back by the hook and answered in S1 (l.22–50). The spiral has no strong "wrong"
    note of its own. Its cold is explained away as ordinary to that flank of the mountain.
  - Emotional pull 2/3. It is a sensual, physical register (crushed thyme, wet sandal straps), which suits a heat chapter.
  - Voice 2/2. The exactness is Mara's: "one, then three together, then a long space of nothing".
  - Connection 2/2. Dawn answers dawn. Ch.14's cliffs come "clear of the night" in gray, and Ch.15's summit takes the sun in
    gold. Nothing is recapped, and the time jump is felt rather than announced.
- **Rewritten:** no.
- **Alternatives considered (not applied):**
  - QUESTION: open on "It was July now, by my own count, which nobody else on the mountain kept." (est. 7). It breaks the
    ledger's `long-spiral` and the outline's opening strategy. It also reads as VOICE, which is barred, and the shape
    detector would count "It was …" as `scene-set`.
  - IN MEDIAS RES: open on `"Is it hot again?" I called down.` (est. 6). It computes as `dialogue`, and it gives the first
    line to a mule's foot.
  - SENSORY, but with an ominous note added inside the spiral, such as the cold read as the door's cold (est. 7). That
    would be a manufactured unease. It would also use the door's cold as a hint, which comes close to the no-foreshadowing
    law. Dialogue-polish kept this sentence byte-identical on purpose.

## Ending (Pull)

> Felix had turned over in his sleep. He lay on his back now, snoring, with his skinned shins to the coals and one arm flung
> over the grass toward the fig's jar, and his lips had swollen fat and shining in the ember light. In his open hand lay one
> of Valens's green figs, with one bite taken out of it.

- **Type: EMOTIONAL GUT PUNCH (quiet, comic-tender).** The writer called the close a "quiet image", which is a ledger move
  and not a pull type. Typed against the six pull types, it is this one.
  - It pays three things without pointing at any of them. The morning's dare ("Eat one and find out."), with "It takes the
    skin from your mouth" now visible on his lips. Felix flinching only when he thought nobody was looking (l.450), and
    "Not in front of everybody": he tested the green fig alone, in the dark, and went back to sleep with it. Valens's
    guard against the wrong thief ("He'll be over those stones some night", said of a sixty-year-old). It was the boy,
    while the adults were in the hut.
  - The order works. The swollen lips come first, unexplained, and the bitten fig explains them in the last sentence. The
    last sentence is shorter and more concrete than the one before it, so the energy tightens at the end.
- **Why it is not DECISION POINT (barred).** No choice is made or enacted in the last 3–5 sentences. The bargain ("As man
  and wife", "Corinth has clerks") is settled pages earlier and is not restaged here. Mara leaving the bed is not a choice
  a reader could argue about. The final image belongs to Felix, who chooses nothing on the page.
- **No aphorism, no foreshadowing.** The close states no meaning and reaches no further than the moment. "toward the fig's
  jar" is a direction, not a pointer. style_check reports foreshadow 0.
- **The paragraph before it (l.610)** is a gentle landing: stars, a black summit, the string asleep, embers. The ledger
  wants quiet here, and the Felix image tightens the energy again. Its black summit inverts the opening's gold one, but
  that symmetry is not the final note, so it is not the reflexive bookend the anti-pattern warns about. Left alone.
- **Score: 7/10.**
  - Forward pull 1/3. The image settles; it does not drive. The Midnight Test passes on the chapter's last pages rather
    than on this image: "She'll be in it." (Plautia in the house), "Then I'll wait outside it," "Corinth has clerks," and
    his disbelief left unresolved. The morning will also find a bitten fig. The ledger chose quiet, and forcing more onto
    it would break the register.
  - Emotional charge 2/3. The tenderness comes entirely from specifics, and Mara has no reaction line.
  - Unpredictability 2/2. Most readers will have filed away the dare, so the shining lips arrive as a surprise.
  - Promise keeping 2/2. The chapter keeps its promises: the cave, the truth, the bargain, the first union, the dare. Its
    open threads (Corinth, Plautia, the disbelieved door) pass to Ch.16.
- **Rewritten:** no.
- **Alternatives considered (not applied):**
  - QUESTION PLANT, closing on `"Here you say it before witnesses, and then everybody eats. Corinth has clerks."` (est. 7).
    It computes as `dialogue` and breaks the ledger's `quiet`. It loses the dare's payoff, and its marriage subtext would
    read as a decision point (barred).
  - OMINOUS PROMISE, anything that leans toward morning or the door (est. 5). Pointing forward is exactly what the
    no-foreshadowing law forbids.
  - The same type with a reaction from Mara added after the fig (est. 5). That is an emotional temperature report and
    would undo the restraint that makes the image work.
  - Compressing l.610 (cutting "The string slept on its feet…") (est. 7, no gain). It is tighter, but it removes the beat
    of the dark hillside that sets up the ember light. The ending is above threshold, so it was not touched.
