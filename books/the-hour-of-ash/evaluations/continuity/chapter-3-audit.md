## WARNING ISSUES

### [W-01] "I stepped out into October." asserts a month the page has not let her infer
- **Category:** timeline / knowledge horizon (foundation §6a; voice-dna §0 Writer's test; STATE canon: she must not know the place-in-time)
- **Evidence:**
  - chapter-3.md:175 "it reached far into the cave in a long slant, yellow going toward orange"
  - chapter-3.md:179 "The sun stood over the ridge to the west, low and swollen… the half still lit had gone a paper-bag brown that I put down to the light. The air coming in under the arch had a chill on it… Woodsmoke… Dung"
  - chapter-3.md:181 "Lichen did not grow like that in an afternoon."
  - chapter-3.md:183 "I stepped out into October."
- **Problem:** the cues Mara registers are low sun, a chill, woodsmoke, dung and lichen. They tell her it is late in the day and that years have passed; a May evening at 1,300 m would give her the same chill and smoke. The one strong seasonal cue, the browned plateau, she explicitly attributes to the light. The strongest cue available to her is never checked: the summits she saw at l.101 "streaked with old snow". So the last line names a month as fact against the evidence the narration has just presented. The hook pass argued from "a brown plain that was green that morning", but the text has her reject that reading. The line also pre-empts Ch.4's planned beat ("Builds on Ch.3: the wrong season, confirmed").
- **ERROR or INTENT:** INTENT by the writer and the hook pass ("perception"). As written it reads as narrator knowledge, so treat it as an ERROR of support, not of canon. October is canon-consistent: news of Jerusalem's fall would reach Delphi then, and Ch.6 is November.
- **Suggested fix (no rewrite here):** let one cue register as *season* before the line. For example, the brown stops being "the light", or the summits are bare where they were streaked at l.101. Alternatively, keep the felt-season but make the perception explicit. The ≤7-word `punch` must survive.
- **Cascade:** Ch.4's "confirmed" beat; Ch.5's "olive-pressing season" (N-11).
