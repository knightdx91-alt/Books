## WARNING ISSUES

### [W-01] The descent reverses Ch.3's route: pen first, then the firs
- **Category:** object/geography continuity
- **Evidence:**
  - chapter-3.md:29 "A little higher there was a shepherd's pen beside the path"
  - chapter-3.md:31 "Above the pen the path went into the firs."
  - chapter-3.md:101 "Above the firs the slope opened out again into broken rock and thyme"
  - chapter-4.md:131 "The path was only a goat track now, beaten pale through dead thyme."
  - chapter-4.md:133 "The pen stood where the pen had stood that morning. I knew it by the bend of the track"
  - chapter-4.md:139, :149 "when the pen was behind us" / "Halfway down"
  - chapter-4.md:163 "At the edge of the firs he stopped. He turned his back on the plain and swung his stick downhill between the trunks"
- **Problem:** in Ch.3 the order going up is plateau, goats, pen, firs, open thyme slope, cave. Coming down the same track (the bend identifies it), she should pass through the firs *before* the pen. Ch.4 passes the pen with no trees, goes "halfway down", and only then reaches "the edge of the firs". The boy then turns his back on the plain to point downhill, which reads as if they had come down past the pen toward the plain and then stopped somewhere uphill of it.
- **ERROR or INTENT:** ERROR (staging), unless these are a different stand of firs on the Delphi side. If so, the page does not say so.
- **Suggested fix:** either put the firs above the pen on the way down, with shard 8's stop at their lower edge, or mark shard 8's trees as a different stand reached after the boy leaves the plateau track for the Delphi side. Keep "I knew it by the bend of the track".
- **Cascade:** Ch.5 opens on the descent to Delphi. Whatever stand of firs ends Ch.4 is where Ch.5's walk starts. Ch.6 climbs back up to the cave.

### [W-02] Mara acts on "this is antiquity" before the page lets her conclude it

- **Problem:** the outline's subtext ("She is trying to ask what year it is") means she is meant to suspect, and that is fine.
