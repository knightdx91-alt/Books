## Changes by conversation

### Valens's account (B1)
"Darker with what?" → "Their feet." These replace "How many ways out?" / "One. The way in's the way out."
- Mara now asks a materials question, which is her axis.
- Valens answers literally and then volunteers the exits himself: "There's one door. I looked for another."
- Attention is back on the right speakers, and the second general-truth saying is gone.

The questions Mara keeps now have reasons of her own:
- **"Nothing else? In the whole room?"** She cannot believe the room is that bare. The answer is still "Smoke. Men.".
- **"Did you hear her own words, or only the voice?"** She wants the god's words, not the priest's.
- **"Surely the priest heard it."** She is pushing for the lost word. Valens answers with the priest's hand, so the hand demonstration is now motivated.
- **"Had you?"** This asks about him, and "I told him the goat was hers." keeps its weight.
- **"Did anybody stop you, coming out?"** Her fear of Celer's men. The answer is literal: "Praxias, in the porch."

The priest's own-account question is folded into Valens's speech as "At the end he asked had I anything…". The witness's chronology now runs unbroken.

### Theano (first live appearance): a voice distinct from every other speaker
Her axis is her own body, the priests, and the god as an employer. She gives no comfort and no explanation. Her rhythm runs long complaint, short flat answer, back to the complaint.
- **"Sit down; my neck won't go back that far."** Her imperatives are now about her own body, which separates her from Kallia's labor imperatives.
- **"Which is worse for them," I said, "the steps down or the steps up?" / "Down," said Theano, to the crows. "Up, I can lean on the wall."** This replaces the "They ache" echo. It gives the card's flat one-word answer and then the return to the complaint.
- **"They put a mountain in it,"** replaces the "mountain out of it" idiom pun.
- **"That's not what you climbed my street for."** replaces "Ask what you came to ask.", which cuts one "Ask" and makes the deflection proprietary and grumbling.
- **"Keep your silver. Whatever's paid on this hill goes to the priests, and I get the steps."** replaces "You'll want it for a boat.". The old line was helpful advice and sat next to Kallia's boats; the new one is a refusal made of grievance.
- **"You've had your answer to that," said Theano, watching the crows. "Ask the other."** This replaces the repeated "Not mine to give you", the third knee-press of the scene, and "in exactly the voice she had used about the stool". The withheld WHEN is still said once, canonically: "That's not mine to give you." … "Or it was never given me."
- **No catchphrase.** "the square beard" is ×2 in her mouth and "my husband went" ×2. Both are under the gate, but they must not reach a third use if she returns.

### Comprehension rule (Mara follows slow, plain speech and loses fast talk)
**The jug boy** was tagged "fast", yet Mara caught every word. **Theano's long complaint** is now paced in body terms: "and went on slowly, taking her breath where she could get it." I deliberately avoided the spent "I followed all of it" move.

- Kallia: short lines to a lodger of five months.
- The walk-ons: one or two sentences each.
