## Changes Made
### Subtext (the Rome argument, kept beneath the words)
- **Hers (will he ever stand still in the open).** Cut "You have not stood still since Corinth" / "I've stood still in this street two years" / "With your back to the wall", because it said the subtext outright. It is now carried by displacement onto evidence from Ch.19's canon page: "Our first night under this roof you asked me which gate was nearest, and then you tried the bar on that door, twice, before you had so much as unrolled a blanket." Valens answers literally, "The door was sticking.", then gives way with "There's always a way out. I find it first." Her closing "let him look" still carries it.
- **His (does she need him to be somebody).** New exchange after "I believe you anyway." He says "And after it I come home and cart stone, the same as now." She answers with over-specificity: "You cart stone. I beat the dust of it out of your tunic every week." She answers the question he did not ask without stating it, and his unspoken word later ("his mouth began a word and gave it up") still stands.
- **Valens's Rome speech.** The third-person, reflective "a muleteer called Valens… nobody in this town had looked at him twice" is recast as plain fact in his own attention: "Here it's scratched on one pier, low down, in a boy's letters." Also: "every idler in the basilica" replaces "men with nothing better to do"; "any man on those benches with a mind to sell it him" puts his worldview (close enough to know you is close enough to sell you) in his own words. A draft "the rain will have it off by spring" was rejected because predicting is Nicanor's bleed-guard axis, not Valens's.
- **Mara's speech.** The anaphora triad "Nicanor did. Fabius did. Felix will…" becomes "Nicanor knew you by your heel. Felix will have told every boy in the barracks before the Kalends." The belief triad "Plautia believed / her brother believes / I believe" becomes "Plautia believed you, and her brother is paying your road on the strength of it. I believe you." No speech about honor anywhere.
- **"His freedman left a sixth of it on my plank"** (a quantifying device) becomes "His freedman put the earnest down on my plank so that a customer's cup rang." This is Mara over-specific about an object while she braces for his reaction.
