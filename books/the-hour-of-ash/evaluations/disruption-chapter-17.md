## Strongest Disruption
**Taking the competence out of the cord-work.** There were three moves: the countdown cut, the narration's "anybody who saw it would know" cut, and the last hole missed twice. Together they turn the theft from a procedure Mara performs into one she barely gets through. The botched knot then reads as the natural end of that, not as a flourish. It also leaves the storeroom sentence as the single moment of real skill, which is what the outline's writer warning asks for. A close second is cutting Valens's three wedged beats in S4: his lines now come out flat and continuous, which is his register under pressure, and the run arrives faster.
