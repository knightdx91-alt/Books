- **"He asks it before he asks the fare,"** answers "Is that all it is?" by not answering. Technique: deflection.
- **"You never told him otherwise," / "He never asked."** comes after Mnason's "Your man's shaved". Valens doesn't deny being her man. It also keeps Mnason's never-asks evasion and sets up the hut. Technique: deflection.
