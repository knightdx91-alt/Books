## Ending (Pull)
- **Type:** DECISION POINT. Not QUESTION PLANT ✓ (Ch.29 was QUESTION PLANT).
- **Score:** 7/10 (Forward pull 2/3, Emotional charge 2/3, Unpredictability 1/2, Promise 2/2). The threshold is 7.
- **Rewritten:** yes, the last sentence only.
- **Original closing:** *"…Valens was a long ridge of wool on the wall side. Whether he slept I had no way to tell.* / *I hoped the next would shake harder."*
- **New closing:** *"…Valens was a long ridge of wool on the wall side. Whether he slept I had no way to tell.* / *I did not wake him."*
- **Why it works:**
  - It follows from the chapter's own run of withholding, and each step tightens:
    1. "He put no question to me."
    2. The answer she had ready and "did not need".
    3. "Valens in the road I left out."
    4. Now, with him perhaps awake beside her, she chooses not to speak.
  - The choice is debatable, which is what a decision pull needs. Telling him would be honest, and he has earned it; not telling him keeps the secret she has kept for years. The reader takes a side.
  - The line stays inside the bed and the night. It does not name the door or the mountain, and it does not reach forward.
  - The sentence before it ("Whether he slept I had no way to tell.") keeps the writer's unreadable sleep. The new line turns that uncertainty into an act of hers, so the ending is not a question.
  - The last sentence (5 words) is shorter and tighter than the one before it (9 words). The energy compresses rather than fades.
- **Promise (2/2):** the line keeps the chapter's promise (the door wanted, then refused) and opens a new one: the silence between them about a place he now knows. Ch.33's argument about whether the door would take the children pays that debt honestly. It is a silence in the present, not a hint.
- **Unpredictability (1/2):** after "I left out", a reader can half-expect a further silence. That is acceptable. The force is in the compression, not in surprise.
- **Midnight test:** pass, as a quiet pull. The reader is left in a bed with two people who both know something and are not saying it.
- **Not the banned "going to sleep" ending:** nobody falls asleep. The line is a choice made toward a man who may be awake.
- **Alternatives considered (not used):**
  - REVELATION, "He was breathing too evenly for sleep." (7). It is strong, but it answers the uncertainty the writer protected on purpose ("Resisted: a line from Valens in the dark"). It would also contradict "no way to tell" in the next breath.
  - EMOTIONAL GUT PUNCH, "I lay down against his back." (6). It reads as reconciliation, which softens the withholding the chapter has built.
  - First draft, "I lay down and did not wake him." (7). It tripped the 5a tic gate: "i lay down" would have been its 3rd use book-wide (Ch.15, 16, 30). The cut to "I did not wake him." is shorter and better.
  - OMINOUS PROMISE was excluded on principle (no foreshadowing; see the ruling).

## FORESHADOWING RULING: "I hoped the next would shake harder."
**Ruling: borderline, leaning (b). It is legal word by word, but as the chapter's last line it works as a wink at the eruption. Replaced.**

**The case for (a)** is real, and it is why this is borderline rather than a clear violation:
- The line passes §0's sentence test: *could Mara have thought this at that exact moment, knowing only what she knew then?* Yes. Fenn told her in Ch.25 that the doors open "when the ground moves hard". The outline's emotional surprise is her relief at the shake.
- It has none of the banned forms in families A–D, and it does not name the mountain or the year. §0 "What is KEPT" item 4 lets the reader supply the irony from their own Vesuvius knowledge.

**Why it falls on the (b) side:**
1. **The wish has already been made in the scene, tied to the door.** "I watched the crack and wished it wider." (line 62) is the legitimate version. It sits next to Fenn's rule and the wall, and it leads straight into the run to the tomb. Repeated at the chapter's last line, the wish is cut loose from the door. It is no longer about the crack or Eros's door. It is about *the earth shaking harder next time*. The writer warning for this chapter says: "her thought is about the door, not the eruption." The closing line is about the ground.
2. **"The next" points forward.** The sentence points ahead to a future shake. Mara knows AD 79 (Ch.14) and knows the doors move with the ground (Ch.25), so the next *hard* shaking she can expect is the mountain's. For her, the thought cannot be fully separated from the eruption. That makes it a forward reach. §0 allows the year as a present thought at most once per chapter and **"never as narration of her own fate."** A last-line wish for the earth to shake harder comes close to the second.
3. **What the line does as a closing button.** A short punch line is the chapter's final note, so whatever it points at becomes the takeaway. Here it points at the reader's own knowledge of what the next big shake will be, and the effect is "be careful what you wish for". That is the OMINOUS PROMISE pull type. Hook-craft has kept that type out of this book on principle since Ch.21. The Ch.29 report ruled that "a pull that leaned toward the tremor would break the no-foreshadowing law". The same reasoning applies to a pull that leans toward the eruption.
4. **Family E's purpose.** §0 says the reader's irony about the ash "belongs entirely to the reader". In the middle of a chapter, the reader can find that irony alone. Put on the chapter's button, the line hands the irony to the reader. That is the "device as a scene-button" the retired-reflex list bans.
5. **The outline's beat subversion** is "for the heroine it is a burst of hope, and for everyone else a plastering job." The chapter already delivers that through the crack, the run, the plasterer's queue and the bread. It does not need to close on the ground.

**Precedent for later chapters (Ch.31–35, before she reckons openly at Ch.36):** a wish or a hope from Mara in the present is allowed when it is tied to a concrete object in the scene (the crack, the door, a hand on paint). A wish about future *ground movement* placed as a chapter button is not allowed.
