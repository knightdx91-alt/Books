## Changes Made

### Subtext (surface: snow, money, the shovel · real: she asks to be believed without saying what; he refuses to be the one who gets involved)
The defined layer was already running. These edits keep it under the surface:
1. **"Whoever knows where can be asked where" → "I don't want it in my head when they come."** The old line made his worldview into a maxim, a character speaking in theme. The new line is a plain personal practicality: he will be questioned and wants nothing to give. The refusal stays the same and the aphorism is gone. (Technique: displacement into physical consequence.)
2. **"Your jar or hers?" → "Most of a pine, then."** The original was a quip, the closest thing in the chapter to meet-cute banter. Now he converts her measure into his own consumable (torches), which sets up "Two more pines after this one" and keeps his attention on practical cost.
3. **The recess:** "Down the cliff. By yourself, in the dark, in snow." → **"You came up that stair holding the gray's tail."** Instead of restating her plan in skeptical fragments (a shape he also used at "So you walked over the mountain. In the autumn. …"), he states one fact about her footing and lets her infer the rest. She answers the fact with the thing she will not explain ("I shall not need to come down"). "Nobody crosses the mountain in this" → "You won't get over the top in this": his misreading (that she is running over to Boeotia) is now carried in a plain forecast.
4. **"That's all I'm asking you."** In book Greek her plea becomes "Only that." She still asks for trust ("Stand there and hold the light and give me so much") and never says what she expects. His answer, "It's not your shovel.", stays deadpan, so the refusal goes through an object.
5. **The "Not past" antiphon cut.** "Forward. Till it stops." / "Till it stops. Not past." / "Not past." became "Then wind it. Every morning." / **"Forward. Till it stops, and not past."** The three-line echo was a shared ritual: a romance signal and a callback forming inside a single chapter. One line from him is enough to show he kept the rule.
