## Strongest Disruption
**Cutting "Nobody at home had ever paid me to leave a join where it could be found."**
- It was the chapter's one sentence of meaning. It stood between Zosimus's request and her hands, and it told the reader that a lesson was being taken. The outline's subtext layer says the lesson is taken *without anyone saying it*.
- With it gone, the sequence runs straight on: he asks, he leaves, she works the lamp, and she trims the welt proud and rubs soot into it.
- The change in her craft is now visible only in what her hands do against the stewpot and the bowl earlier in the chapter. Valens's "Left like that." becomes the only comment anyone makes, and it is a question about her, not the theme.
