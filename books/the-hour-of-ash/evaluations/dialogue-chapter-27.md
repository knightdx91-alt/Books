## Changes Made

### Fenn: lectures trimmed, loneliness kept under the surface
- **Door rules (the bounce).** The rules speech ("It never used to, you'd stand right here and say who you were…") becomes a memory: "I used to stand right here and tell it my name, out loud, like roll call…". He used to say his name to a wall every month, and the line never says why. "you'd feel it on your face before you reached the passage's end" is cut, so the rule comes through Mara's echoing voice and his taps.
- **What killed it.** The line "I went over everything I had a hundred ways" is cut, along with its echo. The new version: "nobody on this coast felt a thing, I asked the length of it for a year. It just quit. One month it's breathing and the next it's a wall with my mark on it."
- **The new day-it-died exchange**, which replaces cut speech: "And you? That morning?" / "Sat out on that terrace till the sun went. The keeper had all his hair then. He brought me out some wine and never asked what for, and I drank it." In twenty-six years the one person who has seen him through anything is a ticket-taker.
- **The FLAT line.** "Every guy who ever found anything wants somebody to show it to" becomes "Carter finds King Tut, and does he sit on it? He sends for somebody to come look." "not a soul in the empire knows it but me" would have been the third "nobody … but me" (cap 2), so it is now "the only folks in the empire who know it are standing in it." "there's more than two of them / mine and yours" is cut as rule-statement.
- **Tarquin** is cut by about a third. "it's never the stuff they kept" read like a theme, so it becomes the treasure-hunter's concrete version: "Nobody ever went digging for the stuff they kept."
- **The Avernus tour** loses "cut the woods, dug the channel, the works" ("the works" was 1 in Ch.26). "about a hundred years late" (an echo) becomes "a century late".
- **The coat** is tightened. "He shook his head at himself" is cut (a head-shake beat). "Thirty-five years gone, and I still think about that coat, it'd come to me in the snow, it'd come to me in Phocis…" becomes "Not a stitch of it. Every winter since, soon as the wind gets in under a door, there it is…". The door is anyone's door, never his. "long enough for" (an echo) becomes "till I could trust it".
- **The addresses.** "By the harbor" (an echo) becomes "Near the boats". "I still came up, now and again" is cut; the line is now "After that it's sixty-two, and Puteoli…". The order is a hut under one door, a room overlooking the next, Misenum an hour from it, then Puteoli for Eros's. None of it is stated.
- **Funny and young, added in the cave** (replacing cut speech): "Come on up yourself, then, there's room for two in here, best seat in the house" (Mara stays on the step: she feels old and the line doesn't say so); "Let him run, he's been riding in a basket all day like a sack of mail"; after the slide, "Safe by a mile, and if the ump says different he can take it up with the commissioner."
- **Smaller fixes.** "you're the boss" → "till I grow roots" (it doubled "You're the doctor"). "a guy in a magazine" → "a guy on a billboard" (it doubled the Cumae magazine). "in case it thought better of it. It didn't." → "in case it had a change of heart. No dice." ("no dice" is a pool item, 1 of 2 book-wide). "a fishing boat … and that's what I came to show you" → "a guy asleep in his boat, and he's the main attraction".
- **Address and tic cleanup.** "Your mister's hauling" → "And the husband, he's hauling"; "Your mister didn't like this" → "Valens didn't like this" (one "your mister" is left, at the slide). The scraper's and keeper's "my friend" are cut (one left: "If I'm still here, my friend."). "Sure he did, and…" → "He did, and…"; "Sure. Who would." → "Who would."; "Sure he did." → "He would." "What was his line, anyhow, before the mules?" → "What was he, before he took up mules?"
- **His narrated states, lightly.** "he went on to me" and "He went on backward out of the light, still talking" are both the SPENT talking-on state; they become "Fenn said" and "He walked backward out of the light." "He considered the jenny's ears" (an object-look) is cut.
- **Greek register:** "A morning there and an afternoon back" (an echo) → "There by noon and back by lamplighting".

### The keeper: distinct by his life
His honey speech no longer runs in Fenn's "and"-chains. It is now short and flat, with his eyes on the sea: "Honey, if the lady wants it. The Sibyl's own, off the windows. Less of it than yesterday. … I stood on this terrace beating a pot lid at them like a fool. None of them so much as looked round." New, replacing cut speech: Fenn's courtly "I went there once myself. For the pay." / "You came back, at least." At the exit he says "They'll be at Misenum already, living on some admiral's roses." Everything he keeps leaves for somewhere better, and Fenn is the one thing that keeps coming back. Nobody says it.

### Mara
- "One of them's on my front" (an echo) → "The newer one's strapped to my front."
- "He fought. In the arena, at Corinth, before my time" (two echoes) → "He was a gladiator, at Corinth. Long before me."
- The Aornos line is clipped to her precise register: "The Greeks called it Aornos. Birdless…"

### Valens
