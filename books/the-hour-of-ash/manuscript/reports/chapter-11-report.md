## The beats, as ordered by the brief
1. **Praxias** (Kallia's cousin, now named) wants five drachmas, then four, then three and a half. Mara has three. Her all-or-nothing reading ("Kallia had one cousin, and I had just spent him") walks her the forty paces to the yard.
2. **The refusal.** She pays the Ch.9 obol before asking, and he tucks it into his cheek. His reasons come as crowd-reading ("Every man in Phocis with a question, all of them watching each other. A priest writes down the name of whoever goes in."). The board comes up, and he pretends: "I heard it read out." She asks for the watch, and he reads it from the open pouch without taking it out ("The short one's past the nine… The long one's at the two"). It runs fast against the spout's shadow, and "It hasn't stopped." She mends harness anyway. That evening he load-tests her breeching by hanging his whole weight from it. Next morning the awl has been sharpened for her. "For a week I mended his harness."
3. **The slow reveal, in order:**
   - **figs**: Caunian against Chian, whole on a rush against split and pressed. "They don't, in Syracuse." "A wasp likes a thing that keeps still." Then the subtext lands: "At the end of summer I shall not be here." / "Then I'm right."
   - **the lame black**: he refuses the twitch, and his hand is on her neck until she offers the foot herself. "She'd remember it." / "That's why."
   - **he reads**: "Take out the door." He has read her sherd. "Felix can't either. It saves arguing." He then lies to Felix in front of her ("How would I know what it says?") and she keeps the secret.
   - **Felix**: bought off a Cilician on the Kirrha quay and freed "wherever I liked", with no paper, no god and no wall ("What wall?"). That night: "He can say it." / "Paper gets taken off you." / "Who took yours?" goes unanswered.
4. **The near-kiss (non-negotiable), done.** He stops a finger's width short and steps back into the hanging girths. He gives no order and says no line. Her relief comes through her legs and her appetite. His comes as a stretch in the yard that cracks his back. Neither explains.
5. **The agreement (non-negotiable), done.** It comes straight out of the anchor, and he gives no reason: "I'll take it in. The day he first sits this year. His birthday, the priests say." (paraphrase; "the seventh of Bysios" is not used). He answers "Why?" with "What name does the priest write?", and she gives "Mara". The watch is **not** returned. The goat: "He'd want more than you have if you had twice as much. At Kirrha there's a man brings them over from Aigion on the boats, at half his price…"

- **Emotional anchor:** hit, at line ~536. Felix is asleep in the end stall against the black's belly, "mouth ajar, and the sack he had been sent to change still in his fist". The black, who kicked his bucket over every evening, "was standing square on all four feet, the bad one as well, which she had not done since it went lame, because to take her weight off it she would have had to shift, and he was asleep against her." This is the chapter's "close the book" sentence, and I placed it on purpose between the refused kiss and the yes.
- **Emotional surprise:** Mara is relieved, and the relief carries no intensity word: "my knees put me onto it… and I was hungry. I wanted bread, and whatever Kallia had in the pot, and I wanted it now."
