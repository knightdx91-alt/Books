## Where each beat landed
1. **Goats, a boy of about nine, staring.** Shard 1 has the goats' heads coming up and the kid that won't stop scratching. The boy comes up off the ground round the corner of the arch. Shard 2 is his entry: sling, knife-cut hair, crust under his nose, the stick kept low, the apotropaic spit into his tunic.
2. **Attic vs Phocian farce.** Shard 2: her fully aspirated "Khaire" comes back as "Khére" and she does not recognize her own word. She panics into English ("There was a man with poles"). Shard 3: she asks the year the Attic way (who is archon?) and gets "Kleodamos" and the month "Boathoos", both useless to her. She answers "Out of the cave" to *ek tou antrou*, which is what makes him decide what she is. She writes ΔΕΛΦΟΙ in the dust and he rubs it out with his foot ("Mē graphe"). She takes *nümfi* for his name and calls him by it, and he laughs himself into a cough.
3. **Granola bar split, spat out.** Shard 5: "strangers and beggars are from Zeus. Have you bread?" He bites, spits, says *Pheu*. She eats both halves. He gives her cheese with goat hair on it ("*Phage*"). He covets the foil wrapper and she gives it to him; it goes down his tunic where the spit went.
4. **The plateau changed.** Shard 6 (see Deviations): the pen stands where the pen stood that morning, its herringbone whole and the corner unrebuilt, with no flat courses and no bedspring. The gate is woven brush tied with withy. The huts are roofed with brush and stones. Grass grows where the road was, and the ridge has only sky on it. The three absences are written in three different syntaxes, so there is no triad.
5. **NON-NEGOTIABLE, *nymphē*.** Shard 8: he points her down to Delphi ("Delphous. Katō, katō"), the one word she hears. She asks his name. He sends the stick up to the cave and brings it round to her breastbone. She asks him to say it slowly, and the third try surfaces letter by letter (nu, a tight upsilon, mu, the phi from the inscription, a wrong eta). The chapter ends on his line.

## Anchor and surprise
- **Anchor:** the word. On the page it is spelled "Nümfi" for the two tries she cannot parse and "Nymphē" for the third, when she hears it. The spelling change carries the moment, and the penultimate paragraph shows the letters forming. The inscription beside the arch, ΝΥΜΦΑΙΣ, fresh and red-filled, is the same root she read worn and broken in 1998, so she has *read* the word and still cannot *hear* it.
- **Surprise (hunger, not wonder):** shard 4 is entirely body. "The hunger arrived all at once," knees going, the smell of his bag. After that point the asides stop (voice-under-pressure level 1→2). The only intrusion is a one-line Wawa hoagie case, a hunger-driven irrelevant thought. Hunger returns in shard 7, and her stomach makes the goats turn round.
- **Beat subversion:** there is no awestruck first look. The ancient world arrives as goats, a pen built the way it always was, a granola bar spat onto the path, and a child who dislikes her snack but wants its wrapper, and who mistakes her for a minor goddess because she said yes when asked if she came out of the cave.

## Chaos moments
- **Mara:** the body rebels (hunger, eyes running at the salt). Under pressure she drops into panic English. There is one irrelevant thought (the Wawa). Her all-or-nothing appears only lightly, in giving the wrapper to the past without comment. The earlier shards keep one dry aside each (Theophrastus, *Useful Phrases*); they die at the hunger, as designed.
- **The boy (secondary):** his own moment is the thorn in his heel, dug out with another thorn while he grumbles at a goat called Kissa and shows off the thorn ("*Ide*"). He also goes still on finding his face in the foil.

## Impulse deviations
- **Kept:** "Out of the cave." In the outline she never confirms where she came from. The text wanted her to answer the one word she caught, and that gives the ending its cause: he calls her *nymphē* because she told him.
- **Kept:** the knucklebones on the ledge under the inscription, where in 1998 she balanced her lens cap, with her hand shut on her own knucklebone in her pocket. It is an object, not reflection, and it is unremarked.
- **Kept:** "Késar" over his shoulder in answer to "Who is Kaisar?". She does not recognize it and nothing is dated; the reader may catch it.
- **Cut:** a "Ti?" answer to "Where's the road?" and another to "Kaisar". Three uses of "Ti?" would have formed a spoken refrain, so it is held to one.

## Outline deviations
- **Beat 4:** the outline's "refuge hut is now a stone pen" has no refuge hut to look back to (ENTITY_STATE CH3-3). I used the prose antecedent instead, the 1998 herringbone pen with the rebuilt corner and bedspring hinge, which now stands whole. "No road. No pylons." is rendered as grass where the road crossed and a ridge with only sky on it (the ski lifts), never as a triad.
- **Pointing down the mountain:** this happens in shard 8, before the word. It does not end the chapter.
- **Nothing dates the year.** The archon name, the month and "Késar" all fail to date it. No 79, no Vespasian, no forward gesture.
