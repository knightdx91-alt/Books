- **Emotional surprise: Fenn young, Mara old.** It is carried by action:
  - Fenn stamps on the hot ground to make it boom, sits in a light-window over the sea with his legs swinging, and slides into home plate.
  - He begs "One more inning, come on, Mom".
  - Mara meanwhile: "I was a little short of forty; Fenn was nearly seventy." … "of us three who were awake in that corridor, only I wanted to go home." The young man drains out of him on the walk back, and his sentences shrink to fragments.
- **Beat subversion:** the place of power is dead, and it isn't even the famous spot. The Sibyl's chamber is "for the customers". The door is a blind storage cut where the keeper keeps spare hive baskets. Fenn brought her to show off a find ("You think Carter dug up King Tut and kept it under his hat?"). He has no idea he is the lesson.
- **Subtext, never said:** his tour is a list of addresses, each measured from a door. A hut under the Corycian, the rope-walk room at Cumae ("I'd check the hill in the morning before I checked the weather"), Misenum, then Puteoli "for Eros's". The keeper's "You say that every year" lands on it without naming it. No "don't end up like me" from anyone.
- **Beats delivered:**
  1. **The invitation, in person, in fresh words, at the start** (Ch.26 cascade). On the wharf with Aelia held at arm's length: "Then tomorrow you're mine, the three of you … We're taking a walk to Cumae." / "I'm showing you, I'm not telling you." No letter carries it. Mara's earlier note said only that they were bringing a load and he might see the girl.
  2. **The road.** The hot ground is texture only: the fullery's smell traced to the sulphur scrapers, the hollow boom, Grata's bronze crescent tucked away from the fumes. The narration never connects the steam to the mountain, and the mountain is never on the page. After that come the Lucrine oyster stakes, then Avernus with its silted navy yard and its ducks ("So much for that.").
  3. **The named frame.** Fenn on the road in daylight trying to light the dead lighter for the picture: a grass stalk for a cigarette, two thin sparks, "it works sometimes, you've got to warm it up". Mara takes the frame "while his thumb was still trying". This is the lighter's one appearance, the first time it is called a Zippo, and the first time it is shown dead. The frame she wanted of Lucius and the ducks is lost.
  4. **The long trapezoidal gallery, cut light (NON-NEGOTIABLE).** The walls lean in, wide at the floor and narrow at the top. The pick strokes run in slanting rows. Bars of sun come in from the seaward light-passages, with the sea's noise at each opening.
  5. **Fenn finds the dead door; the hand at the threshold is his.**
  6. **Doors can die, shown not lectured.** Mara's voice bounces off the rock and Fenn's taps answer; at a live door both used to go in and stop. A live door is cold before you reach it; this one is "warm like a wall's warm". It died in the spring of AD 50 with no shake and nothing on the tables. He sat up with it every Kalends for a year, then once a year in that month.
  7. **The crossing story, more of it: the coat.** He slept in it at the Corycian mouth, took it off because you can't get down the slot on your belly in a coat, folded it on the rock where the string was tied, and put the notebook in the pocket so it wouldn't get wet. He landed in snow in shirtsleeves. He believes a goatherd got the coat. Mara has the consulate carbon in the notebook's back pocket and keeps it from him: "In his room he had said he knew what was in the notebook. That, he had no notion of."
  8. **Lucius throws the knucklebone down the gallery; Fenn draws a baseball diamond in the dust (NON-NEGOTIABLE).** The bone's verbs avoid rolled / fetched / skittering / flat side / palm. Richie Allen goes on third. Lucius stops exactly on each bag. Fenn runs the bases and slides.
  9. **The long fade on the walk back, the bay going purple.** Felix is not mentioned (news on return, Ch.28).

  - **Valens is not on the walk.** He hauls brick to Misenum that day; the outline lists only Fenn, Mara, Lucius and the baby. He appears in Puteoli in §1 and at the dawn yard. Four mules, unchanged.
  - **Felix is unmentioned.** He was not on the trip, so his absence can be news on return (Ch.28).
  - **Lucius rides a hired jenny** chosen by Valens. A child of about two cannot walk 12 km each way.
  - **The route detours north to the hot ground** first ("It's on my way"). The ledger asks for Puteoli → the Phlegraean fields → the cave, and the Solfatara bowl is behind Puteoli.
  - **The dead door is not at the Sibyl's chamber** but in a blind side cut. That is the subversion.
  - **The anchor's "as if it might be warm" is rendered as waiting for cold**, to stay consistent with Ch.19 (dormant door = faintly cool).
  - **The Cocceius tunnel is not used.** A dark tunnel with light shafts would pre-spend the gallery's cut light. They take the ridge path.
