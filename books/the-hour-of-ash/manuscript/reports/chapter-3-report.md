## Structure
- **Structural approach:** SINGLE-SCENE, with the internal shape *walk → threshold*. It differs from Ch.2 (Collage) and Ch.1 (Chronological). Delphi, the taxi and the library are backfill inside the walk, never staged.
- **Function:** Reversal, built as escalation. The world changes, and she is the last to know.
- **Hook / pull type:** ACTION UNFINISHED / SENSORY-WRONGNESS. The chapter stops on her first step into a season she can see but cannot account for. This is neither CONTRAST/QUESTION-PLANT (Ch.1) nor VOICE/EMOTIONAL-GUT-PUNCH (Ch.2). Resolved: is the cold real? Opened: where is she, and why has the paint gone?

## Anchor and surprise
- **Anchor:** the knucklebone. A conservator who wrote the leave-it-in-place procedure herself pockets an ancient worked astragalus (L125–131). The sin is set up by the German's harmless stone for his daughter and by her father's sherds in the *Cyprus Mail*, still kept in a shoebox (L45–51). It is never explained. Her body reacts (ears hot, standing too fast) and she promises herself the museum guard. On the way out she is still holding it in her pocket (L171).
- **Surprise:** at the crossing she feels embarrassed, not afraid. The blush comes up in the cold, the blush of a 1989 department Christmas party in Mount Airy when she opened a bedroom door on eight or nine strangers who all stopped talking. "There was no one here." She has "sorry" in her mouth behind the flashlight and backs out (L163–167). The "as if" was not spent here; the comparison is a concrete memory instead.
- **Beat subversion:** no light, no voices, no terror. The door is cold, quiet and socially awkward. She does not know anything has happened. Hurrying out, she reads none of the walls, and she notices the change only by the light and the missing paint, with the brown plateau rationalized away.
