## CH13-7 (the cave): how it's handled
A substantial backfilled passage of about 800 words, triggered by the cave's patch of shade visible across the plateau at dawn (l.22). It runs into the scene and back out to the fold. None of the cave's spent words are reused: no slab, flowstone, hall, drip, torch relay, palm to the black, or wrist depth. The morning after the consultation, Valens waits below the cliffs with two mules. Snowmelt comes off the cave lip in a glittering sheet. **She brings the flashlight on purpose** (the "any use must reckon with being seen" rule, now reckoned). The beam is yellow, with a brown edge from tiring batteries. **What the crack did: it showed its back wall.** A yard past the bend, gray stone closes it from wall to wall, sweating, with a pale crust and a clot of daddy longlegs coming apart one leg at a time. "That is the end of it." / "Was it ever further?" / "Once." Valens watches what the beam lands on and leaves "the thing in my hand" to her, then says nothing about the light for four months, until noon today. Canon kept: shut for good; the door home is in Pompeii.

## CH14-4 (the Euthydikos bowl): how it's handled
Her Ch.14 plan gives way on the page, inside the bargain. Mara: "I meant to winter in Delphi. Euthydikos has a bowl for me when the god closes for the year. It would have paid my crossing." Valens: "Delphi's put you on a boat. Leave yourself on it." (Kallia told the spring that the Syracusan sailed.) The money is answered: "The brother pays his witness's road. There's the silver from the ford," and some of the string is to be sold at the Isthmus. Then her own image of the bean-sheller at the lane gate in winter and Kallia telling him she sailed, closed with "The priest's bowl could keep its lead; it had kept it this far." Felix, kept out of her plan since Ch.14, goes into it: "He comes."

## The bridge (spring → July)
One paragraph plus Kallia's spoken line. In the month after the consultation, a bandaged stranger "with a Corinthian's way with his vowels" sat at the lion spouts asking which house kept "the tall foreigner with something heavy and bright inside her clothes". Kallia, under a brimming jar, said "Gone… onto a boat for home… She ate more than she carried, that one, and broke two of my jars." She packed Mara's bag that night. Mara reached Valens's yard before light and went up behind the string in a carter's hood when the snow left the high ground. Valens's fig rode up in its sawn-off jar on the dun. This fits his Ch.12 plan for the high grazing ("you see a man coming a whole morning off"), which is not quoted. It also leaves Ch.16's autumn farewell to Kallia possible as a quiet return.

She does the sum aloud ("nineteen hundred years, and twenty-seven more. Twenty-six, and some months. I left in May. It is a month we have; it comes after April." / "I know May."). Then the cave door "for one afternoon", then Theano's town: "Pompeii." The flashlight is shown (his cupped palm lit red along the fingers) and so is the camera ("It keeps what it shows"). He gets the lens end wrong first, and she stops his thumb before it reaches the dented rim (no cap anywhere).
- **She does NOT tell him what happens to Pompeii.** The one AD-79 present thought of the chapter (l.272): "eight years on from this summer, and a few weeks over. I left it standing where it was; his question had been about the road."
