- **Outline deviations and canon decisions:**
  1. **Whose name the priest writes (open item from Ch.11).** Shown without a gloss. The priest's wax reads "Maira, of Syracuse, of the house of Kallia, a kinswoman"; Praxias stands as "Praxias son of Kleon, of Delphi"; and for the carrier, "I gave him Felix." Kleon is a new mention-only name.
  2. **CH12-8 left open.** "Mine's written. … At Corinth." does not say which name is written.
  3. **Why Theano tells more than the god gave.** She never explains it. The cover is her grievance: the priests "put shoes on" what she brings up, and her short answer had a word in it that was not Greek. Valens heard that word twice and "didn't keep it" because it was not in what the priest gave him. On the page that unkept word sets up "Pompeii" without any narrator pointing.
  4. **The priest's own reading.** He reads "Syracuse" off his wax and glosses the answer as Etna. Mara rejects it on geography (Etna faces the eastern sea). Her own guesses are Etruria's painted tombs, Sicily, "the Bay of Naples", and Spain. She never thinks of Vesuvius. The Bay of Naples appears once, in a list, unweighted.
  5. **Theano's tenure.** "Fourteen years… since my husband went", "three of us to take turns" and "Now there's only me" follow Diodorus's over-fifty rule and Plutarch's report that the number of Pythias had fallen to one. Her house is the second from the top of the street (Ch.8's "last gate but one"). She is recognized by situation only, and no Ch.8 line is re-quoted.
  6. **The ibuprofen.** Offered to Valens at the altar first ("After… I want my head, in there"), then given to Theano with instructions, swallowed without thanks, and never mentioned again.
  7. **Kallia keeps Valens the night** ("There's floor"). That sets up Ch.14's sleeper across the doorway without staging the doorway.
  8. **Continuity from Ch.12, all honored:**
     - his graze, welt and knuckles, and his stiff shoulders, are on the page;
     - the goat was at the yard "since sundown yesterday";
     - Kallia gets the whole story at the lions;
