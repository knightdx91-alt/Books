The stool creaked. Somebody was saying Iuno Lucina, Iuno Lucina, over and over in a low voice, and it was Grata.

"Now."

"Not yet. Breathe."

The midwife's breath smelled of onions and her front teeth crossed over each other. I hated her teeth. I hated them with a clean, total hatred that left no room for anything else, and that was a mercy.

Water in a basin. A lamp spitting.

Outside, a hammer. Someone shoeing a mule in the middle of the night, steady, three blows and a pause, three blows and a pause.

"Now."

I did not hear myself. I heard the hammer stop.

Then a sound that was not anyone I knew, thin and furious, rising up out of the room like smoke going the wrong way; and Grata laughing; and the midwife saying something brisk in Oscan; and the hammer outside dropped on stone, and the door.
