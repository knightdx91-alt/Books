## 0. CANON LOCK (do not alter)

**Spelling:** **Sygl / sygl / sygls**, and "sygl-less" for Bal before the hand. Never "sigil" or
"sygil." Also: mausoleum (not "mausaelium"), **Naisura** (never "Waisura"), surprised, imagine.
The book's school is **The Heir's School of Magykal Artes**; the spellbook is **`Sygldry Year 2`**. **Sygldry** is the general
name for the study of sygls and the magykal schools that teach it; the Heir's School is the first and grandest,
the rest are the "lesser schools" (Fredrick went to one). The yearly claiming happens at the Heir's School.

**Names:** Balthazaar "Bal" Norheart; Amelia (Vampire Goddess, originator of the Blood sygl, Bal's
familiar; "Amelia Norheart" on her Dawn card); Viridia Vizer (Tempus, storm inheritance, converted to
Blood); Lora Aegis (Waves, rapier, alchemy, converted to Blood); Mrs. Sarah Brown (orphanage); Verin
(escort mage; horse Thistle); Fredrick (a **friend** who left for school and gave Bal his spellbooks);
originators **Aren/Nera** (Duality), **Kenmei** (Waves), **Naisura** (Terra), **Raizen** (Tempus);
Mr. Porter (familiars; raven Huginn), Mr. Winters (runes), Zoran (warfare); Tyler (a student);
priests **Thad Moren** and **Xavier Giles**; Commander **Fern**; **Eric** (battalion lead); **Marcus**
(weapons master); centurion **Jazen Grey** and his horse **Barda**; **Peter** (built the hidden temple,
died on the altar); **Hopper** (bloodwolf, Viridia's familiar) and Lora's raven; **Sean Kiin** (Tempus
Lead Mage); bandit leader **Heath** (the heart ring) and assassin **Iro**; Raizen's priests **Aleric**
(scar through the right eye), **Titus**, **Leon**; High Priest **Toma** (life-bonded storm elemental
**Akora**); **James** the brewer; **Ms. Summers** the alchemist; **Mrs. Locklan** (Winespring Inn);
**Soren** and **Kim** (Dawn Hall); **Bran** (Viridia's would-be husband); team **"Sygls Best"**; places
New Spring, Whitehaven, Saegen Woods, Kysmer, Londra, Pashar, Cartesia, the Ventish region; the Three
Boars Inn; the Winespring Inn; the Dawn faction; aurora felis; nightstalker poison.

**Physical canon:** sygls appear **on the palm** (Bal's on his left palm after the regrowth; Amelia's
familiar brand on her forehead, which she can show or hide). Bal is sixteen and has an **unusually high
pain tolerance**. Bal is **not** the only orphan without magic: there are others at Saint Andre's who can
do no magic at all; Bal could always do some, just weaker without a sygl.

**World rules that drive plot:**
- Sygls give an affinity; schools and many factions require one; blood sygls are feared and infants born
  with one often have the hand removed. Weak affinities can surface late in life.
- Once a year every originator comes to the Heir's School to claim their own; Amelia's seat has been empty.
- An immortal can be trapped only in one of their own places of power. Eight high priests died to trap
  Amelia for almost 3,000 years, in a temple Naisura's high priest built as a "peace offering."
- Blood mages can alter sygls, never without the person wanting it; only Amelia's **High Priest** can
  convert (cut both left palms, mingle blood, push his blood in until the palm sygl changes). Converts keep
  their inherited gifts.
- Originators may not interfere with another's High Priest. As Bal's **familiar**, Amelia can help him on
  certain things without it counting as originator interference.
- **War rules** (revealed when the source reveals them, Ch.18 and Ch.20): once war is declared no new
  priests or High Priest; the war ends when one side's priests and High Priest are dead; once war is
  declared originators may not help their followers; the victor may demand anything that does not
  physically harm the loser.
- Drinking blood heals Amelia's High Priest and strengthens his blood magyk (regular priests: healing
  only); drinking hers shares her memories.
- A life-bonded elemental from an inheritance gift is not a familiar, so it is legal in a duel. Converting
  the elemental's core converted the life-bonded High Priest. Preserve exactly.

## 1. PREMISE

> A sygl-less, one-handed orphan flies into the sealed temple behind his orphanage and finds the goddess
> the world calls a myth: Amelia, the originator of the feared Blood sygl, locked away for three thousand
> years by the other gods. He regrows his hand on her blood, bonds her as his familiar, and walks her out
> the front door into a school, a found family and a war. As her High Priest he can change a sygl, but only
> for someone who wants it: the girls he loves choose the Blood with him, and in the end he converts the
> enemy High Priest by accident, winning the war Raizen started and the home none of them had.

**Structural premise:** the gods treat their followers as pieces on a board; Amelia stays with hers. Bal,
the boy nobody claimed, becomes the one who claims and protects his own, and the four of them "play their
game and break their toys" until the gods have to leave them alone.

---

## 2. THEME

> **Being wanted for who you are, not for what you can do.**

The source states it plainly and the rewrite keeps it: Bal to Amelia, "I don't want you here because of what
you can do for me, I want you here because I like you"; Bal to her again, "You belong here, with us"; the
gods who see followers as expendable against a goddess who comes to her people. The rewrite **dramatizes**
these beats in full scene; it does not argue with them, undercut them, or add doubt to them.

Supporting threads, all from the source: the outsiders finding each other (Bal, Amelia, Lora, Viridia, Peter);
choosing your own way (Bal's self-study; Viridia's freedom; Lora joining Dawn); justice against those who
threaten the people you love (Iro, the priests, Raizen's sentence).

**Tone:** warm, teasing, adventurous and openly romantic, with real danger. Bal's violence against those who
threaten his girls is justified and the book treats it so. The ending is **unambiguously happy**: "the first
day of the rest of their life together."

**Breathing room:** New Spring (Ch.13), the lake before the ambush (Ch.21), the coda (Ch.30).

## 3. CHARACTERS (source-supported only)

### Balthazaar "Bal" Norheart
**Source facts:** sixteen; mother died giving birth; raised at Saint Andre's; born with a blood sygl and the
hand removed; could still do some magic; taught himself from Fredrick's books; curious, likes secrets and
reading ("I like to read"); worked in the kitchen to buy a rune engraving kit; skilled with runes from the
start; prefers handling his own problems; warm and frank with the girls; protective ("I'd much rather I got
hurt than any of you"); keeps trying to settle things before Amelia kills anyone; refuses to answer Raizen's
persecution in kind; high pain tolerance; juggles stones once (p.103), learned when younger.
**Want:** to attend the school; then to keep Amelia, Lora and Viridia safe and win the war.
**Arc (as the source runs it):** from the boy nobody claimed to the High Priest who belongs and protects his
own: "I just want to live my life with my girls."
**Worldview:** notices mechanisms (how the dorm grows rooms, how meals are ordered, how a rune works) and asks
practical questions ("What can I do to help?"). **Syntax:** plain, short, friendly, a joke under pressure.
**Evasion:** does not dwell on his own hurts; makes light of wounds ("Would you believe I fell on my rapier?").
**Physicality:** the new left hand; stillness and a cold, quiet voice when someone threatens his girls (Sean,
Iro). The source notes once that the surge of anger at Sean surprised him; it is not developed into self-fear.
**Tic line:** none — distinguished by worldview and syntax.

### Amelia
**Source facts:** looks a few years older than Bal; pale, black hair to mid-back, red eyes, black dress, ruby
teardrop earrings; turned at sixteen, killed the farmer who found her while blood-mad; became the newest
originator by consuming magical energy; once wiped out half a battlefield; feared because she won't play the
others' games; trusted them, was betrayed and imprisoned ~3,000 years; carries Peter's memories through his
offerings; explodes into bats when furious (once, ch.2); weeps blood; reads Viridia's diary and may keep her
own; protective, loving, open with her affection, lethal to enemies; needs "a little time" before eating with
the others; "I feel useless" when she can't protect them.
**Want:** freedom; to let her followers know she didn't abandon them; justice on the gods who caged her.
**Arc:** from three thousand years alone to a family that wants her ("Amelia Norheart"); her one concession
is justice: Raizen in her old temple for three thousand years.
**Worldview:** reads people and secrets; treats the gods' wars as games she refuses to play. **Syntax:** teasing,
quick, can drop into calm, complete-sentence menace toward enemies ("Think your next words through very
carefully"). **Evasion:** won't ask directly to be included. **Physicality:** sudden appearances and
departures with a snap; sitting cross-legged; bloody tears.
**Form of address:** may call Bal "Master" (author ruling above).
**Not:** a manipulator of Bal or the girls. The Hopper bond is a kindness Bal admires as clever; nothing she
does to her own is a trap.
**Tic line:** none — distinguished by worldview and syntax ("Master" is a title, not a tic).

### Viridia Vizer
**Source facts:** pale blue crackling hair and matching eyes (storm inheritance, Raizen's rarest gift), a
little taller than Bal; her village planned her life at birth, including marriage to the headman's son
Bran; a sygl was her way out; thinks the originators are "people just like us"; doesn't care about petty
politics; brave to the point of drinking what she believes is poison; her hair crackles when nervous and lies
still when she is frightened; uses a spear; Hopper; the earth ring; glances at jewellery in shop windows.
**Worldview:** reads status games and refuses them. **Syntax:** blunt, dry, teasing; provocations as questions.
**Evasion:** deflects sincerity with a joke until it is too big to deflect (the diary, the poison).
**Physicality:** the hair. **Tic line:** none — distinguished by syntax and physicality. Source lines such as
"I'm not like most people" and the stuck-out tongue are used once, where the source has them, never as a
signature.

### Lora Aegis
**Source facts:** shy; brown hair, glasses that slide down her nose, freckles; Kenmei/Waves sygl; trained by
her father (once in Kenmei's army) and the weapons master; a rapier prodigy who beat every boy in her town's
tournament at thirteen and was mocked for it; two years of training by day and reading by night; wants to join
the Dawn faction; alchemist (the book, the poison, Ms. Summers); loses herself in whatever she's doing (the
three hours helping another alchemist); dispatched three bandits alone; coaches Viridia on noble manners; holds
Viridia through her breakdown; "We'll figure it out, together."
**Worldview:** craft and preparation. **Syntax:** hedged and polite with people, fluent and exact on alchemy,
weapons and history. **Evasion:** turns down what she wants for fear of not being ready ("I need to study
more," said once, as the source has it). **Physicality:** the rapier as an extension of her; glasses.
**Tic line:** none — distinguished by worldview and syntax.

## 7. VOICE

**Narration:** close third on **Bal only**, PAST tense. Plain, warm, curious, conversational; vocabulary of a
bright sixteen-year-old; humour frequent and affectionate; emotion through behaviour and speech. Accelerate in
fights; slow down for set-pieces (the claiming, the card, the lake, the toast, the duel). Under grief (waking
alone, Ch.27) the prose goes short and plain; under threat to his girls it goes quiet and exact. These are
registers, not tics.
**No dual narration in prose.** The "Amelia butts into the narration" device is **manga-only** if the author
wants it there; the novel is Bal's close third throughout.
**Anti-patterns:** see STYLE_CONSTRAINTS (em-dash cap, filter words, stage-business tags, repeated phrases,
"the way [x]" cap, no metacognition, no emotion reports, no present tense, fade-to-black).

**Voice differentiation (four axes, no tics):**

| Character | Worldview / attention | Syntax | Evasion | Physicality |
|-----------|----------------------|--------|---------|-------------|
| Bal | mechanisms, secrets, how to help | short, plain, joking under pressure | his own pain | the new hand; quiet when threatened |
| Amelia | people's secrets; the gods' games | teasing, then calm menace | asking to be included | snaps, sudden arrivals, bloody tears |
| Viridia | status games she refuses | blunt, dry, needling questions | sincerity until it's too big | hair crackles / lies still |
| Lora | craft, preparation, history | hedged with people, exact on her subjects | wanting things she isn't "ready" for | the rapier; glasses |

Test: a line that could belong to either girl is wrong. Viridia needles and does not hedge; Lora hedges and does
not needle.
