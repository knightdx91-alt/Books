# Sygl — Hard Style Constraints (read before writing or editing ANY chapter)

Non-negotiable. Every writer/editor pass must comply, and the evaluator must flag violations.
Revised 2026-10-20 (author: "make it more like my original script. And it's supposed to be Sygl.").

## 0. SPELLING — it is SYGL
- **Sygl / sygl / sygls** everywhere, and **sygl-less** (never "sigil", "sygil", "sigil-less").
- Also fixed: mausoleum, **Naisura** (never "Waisura"), surprised, imagine, Viridia Vizer,
  Balthazaar "Bal" Norheart, The Heir's School of Magykal Artes, `Sygldry Year 2`.
- The source mixes "magic" and "magyk/magykal"; plain "magic" is the narration default, with
  "magyk" kept where the source uses it in names and set phrases. Do not alternate within a scene.

## 1. EM DASHES
- **Hard cap: at most 2 per 1,000 words**; target near 0 (the source uses none). Never two in one
  paragraph; never the "noun — appositive — verb" interrupter as default rhythm. Count after every chapter.

## 2. NO TICS — zero tolerance (repo law since 2026-10-18)
- No character, the narrator included, carries a verbal tic, catchphrase, refrain, pet phrase, signature
  gesture or device (counting, listing, cataloguing, pricing). Distinctness comes from **worldview, syntax,
  evasion and physicality** only. See `/docs/CHARACTER-BIBLE.md`.
- **Single exception (author ruling 2026-10-20):** Amelia may call Bal **"Master"**, a legitimate title from
  a familiar to her bonded mage, where the source uses it or the relationship makes it natural. It is a
  form of address, not a refrain; do not build jokes around repeating it.
- Source lines that occur once ("I'm not like most people," "I need to study more," "That girl loves having
  the last word") are used once, where the source has them, never as signatures.
- No two characters share a device. Exception (author decision 2026-10-20): the source gives a stuck-out
  tongue to both Amelia (p.24, Ch.5) and Viridia (p.35, Ch.7); both are kept, once each. Neither recurs.
- Gestures that recur in the source (Amelia's finger-snap, Viridia's crackling hair) are rendered with variety
  and only where the source has them, never as a signature. Bal flexing the new hand is spent in Ch.3.
- Stage business rationed: `nods/nodded`, `shrugs/shrugged`, `shakes his head`, `grins` as a tag; hair
  tucked behind an ear; anything picked up and set down. Vary or cut.
- Filter words: `just`, `seemed`, `really`, `slightly`, `suddenly`, `began to`/`started to`, `visibly`.
- Dialogue tags: `said`/`asked`, PAST tense.
- Author's dialogue is the BASE, not sacred text (author, 2026-10-20: "my dialogue might not be exactly right"). Keep what each line says, who says it, and where it falls, plus the memorable lines (e.g. "They have made me a myth?!", "Because it's magic", the binding vows, "No one threatens my girls"). Freely fix grammar, stiff or awkward phrasing, and anything that doesn't sound natural spoken aloud. Do not change meaning, add information, or give a line to someone else.

## 3. NO REPEATED PHRASES OR METAPHORS
- No phrase ("for a moment", "look on his/her face", "be able to") recurs as a crutch. Track 3- and 4-grams
  across chapters.
- A metaphor used once is spent for the whole book (e.g. "firelight reflecting off her eyes", spent in Ch.2).
- An image or gesture used as one chapter's Opening/Anchor/Hook is not reused as another's (the outline's
  ledger tracks them; single-use: the softball game, the juggled stones, the tongue-out (once per character), the map under a finger).

## 3b. "the way [x]" SIMILE CAP
- At most 2 per chapter, staggered (0–1 in alternate chapters). Prefer plain syntax.

## 4. TENSE & POV
- PAST tense throughout.
- **Close third on Bal ONLY, no exceptions.** No scene, glimpse or paragraph outside what Bal sees, hears or
  is told. Events he is absent for arrive **reported**, as the source reports them (the bloodwolf bond,
  Amelia's council with the gods, Lora's spar with Marcus, the girls' captivity). Where the source slips
  into another head (the bandit fight, the toast), render what Bal observes and what the character says.
- **No dual narration.** The "Amelia butts into the narration" device is not used in prose (manga-only, if
  the author keeps it there).

## 5. CONTENT & TONE
- Romance/intimacy is **fade-to-black**. Build tension; cut before explicit acts.
- Keep the source's meaning: Bal's violence against those who threaten his girls is justified (Iro hangs from
  the blood whip; "No one threatens my girls"); Amelia is loving and never manipulates her own; the girls
  choose the Blood freely; the ending is unambiguously happy. Do not add guilt, dread, coercion or
  "unsettling" residue the source does not have.

## 6. LENGTH
- Whole novel **≥ 90,000 words** (outline total ≈ 91,856 over 30 chapters: Ch.1–4 as written, 11,756; Ch.5–30
  targets 1,300–5,300 each, shortest ≥ 1,200). Expansion must add scene and substance, not padding or new plot.

## 7. CANON
- Source of truth is `Sygl_Complete.txt`, **in its order**. Preserve every beat, name and world rule;
  `project/foundation.md` §0 is the canon lock. Notable fixes: Mrs. Brown's husband's late affinity was
  **Blood**; sygls are **on the palm**; Bal has a **high pain tolerance**; other orphans have no magic; Fredrick
  was a **friend**. No invented backstories, quirks or motives.
