## Back-cover copy

**Nobody claims a boy born with the Blood sygl.**

Bal Norheart lost his left hand for it before he could crawl. No sygl, no school. That was the rule.

Then the sealed temple behind his orphanage flickers. Bal flies a bedside rug through a roof that isn't there, and finds Amelia: the Vampire Goddess, locked away by the other gods for three thousand years and furious to learn the world calls her a myth.

One drink of her blood, and Bal has a hand again. One binding vow, and the goddess is his familiar. She walks out the front door beside him, straight into the Heir's School of Magykal Artes and the empty throne the gods kept for her.

Now Bal is the school's only Blood student, the High Priest of a goddess everyone fears, and the one mage alive who can change a person's sygl. He can do it only if they want it. Viridia, the storm-haired heir of the god Raizen, and Lora, the shy rapier prodigy, are starting to want a great many things.

And the god Raizen does not share what is his.

The gods play with their followers like toys. Amelia has a better idea: play their game, and break their toys.

*(optional, cut first if space is short)* A warm new adult fantasy of found family, forbidden magic and a love big enough for four.

**Closed-door romance. All romantic leads are 18+.**

### Notes for the designer and author
- The copy ends at the midpoint (the school rule looming, before the conversions). It gives away nothing from the second half.
- "Harem" is deliberately kept off the print cover. The retailer listing carries that signal, where the readers who search for it will find it. "A love big enough for four" and "closed-door" set the expectation honestly on the physical book.
- Keep the content line ("Closed-door romance. All romantic leads are 18+.") even if the optional line goes. It is the honest heat signal on the physical book.
- Spelling on the wrap: **Sygl / sygl** (never "sigil"), **Magykal Artes**.
