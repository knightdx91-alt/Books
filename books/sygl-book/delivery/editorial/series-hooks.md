# Sygl: Series Hooks for Book 2

Threads Book 1 leaves open, all taken from what is on the page (chapter references in brackets). This is a planning list for the author, not a plan. Nothing here is canon until you decide it.

## The five you named

1. **Toma at the Heir's School.** Raizen's former High Priest, 56, is converted to the Blood by accident and now carries its mark on his palm. Aren took him to the Heir's School, where the healers will look after him "until he is well". After that he can decide for himself what a man his age does with the Blood's mark [Ch.30].
   *Open questions:* Is he an enemy, a convert, or something in between? He grieved his three priests [Ch.29]. He also promised to take pleasure in killing Lora and Viridia.
2. **The Dawn faction.** Sygls Best (Bal, Lora, Viridia, and "Amelia Norheart" on the fourth card) is a registered team with ranked cards, jobs and bounties, and rare spellbooks at higher ranks [Ch.13–18]. Lora's closing line: "we've still got school. And the Dawn faction" [Ch.30].
   *Ready-made engine:* new jobs, rank-ups, Soren and Kim at the Dawn Hall, Ms. Summers and alchemy.
3. **The blood elemental.** It formed when Bal forced his blood into Akora's core. "There was also no such thing. They did not exist. They never had." Amelia doesn't understand it either, and she sent it to her domain to study [Ch.29–30].
   *Open questions:* What is it? Is it bonded to Bal? Can he call it?
4. **The conversion power.** It is now public among the gods: Bal can convert a High Priest, and Aren fears he can convert the unwilling [Ch.29]. Bal's compromise is that he converts only people who come asking, and Amelia notifies the originators first. Aren: "the others will not like this … it is not something they will forget." Bal: "there's no telling how far what I did will reach" [Ch.29–30].
   *Pressure points:* Who comes asking? Bal's speech in the hall invited anyone tired of not belonging to join the Blood [Ch.20]. And what does an originator do when one of their own asks?
5. **Raizen in the temple.** He is confined for three thousand years, with no contact with the outside world, in the very temple where Amelia was imprisoned [Ch.29]. That temple stands **behind Saint Andre's orphanage**, Bal's old home [Ch.1].
   *Open questions:* Who leads the Tempus followers now? The Tempus Lead Mage, Sean Kiin, whom Bal threatened to duel [Ch.20], is still at school. The other gods watched one of their own sentenced. Raizen once taught a follower to kidnap and torture a Blood High Priest [Ch.4]. And the locks on that temple work only one way [Ch.3].

## Other open threads on the page

- **The chalice's side effects.** Amelia's blood strengthens Bal's blood magyk and "might give you other blood magyk abilities too", which she called "too unpredictable to tell" [Ch.28]. It's a natural source of new power in Book 2. *Spell-repetition watch:* any new ability should come from the chalice or the blood elemental, not from new invented spells.
- **A second inheritance gift.** Bal told the girls a convert can gain another inheritance gift [Ch.19]. Neither has shown one yet.
- **The other gods.** Naisura's army is still recovering from his attack on Nera [Ch.11]. Kenmei "didn't care either way" about losing Lora [Ch.19]. Both will now weigh a Blood High Priest who can convert.
- **The war's aftermath.** Blood-sygl villagers were beaten, driven out and evacuated [Ch.18, 23, 27]. Commander Fern, Eric's battalion, Thad and Xavier, Jazen Grey, and the hidden canyon temple Peter built [Ch.11] are all still in play.
- **Families.** Lora: "I can't say how my father will feel … my family will stay by me" [Ch.19]. Her father served in Kenmei's army. Viridia's village and Bran, the headman's son she was promised to [Ch.27]. Viridia asked that her village be left alone [Ch.20].
- **Mrs. Brown's husband.** He had a late Blood affinity, was fired, and was shot coming home from work. "I've never been able to find out why, but I've got my suspicions" [Ch.4]. It's unresolved and sits next door to Raizen's new prison.
- **Amelia's diary.** "Maybe. You'll never find it though." Bal resolved to find it [Ch.11]. A light, warm runner.
- **The school.** Aren lifted Porter's no-mingling rule as Amelia's concession [Ch.21]. Mr. Porter and the hostile students are still there, and Bal is still the Blood's only Lead Mage, now with two priests [Ch.19–20].
- **Fredrick.** Bal's friend at a lesser Sygldry school, who gave him the spellbooks [Ch.1]. Bal wanted to write to him and couldn't find a way to begin [Ch.3].

## Endpoint reminder

Book 1 ends unambiguously happy: "For the first time in his life, Bal was exactly where he belonged" (closing line provisional). Book 2 should open from that settled home and threaten it, not undo it.
