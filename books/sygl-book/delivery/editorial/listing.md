## 1. Retailer description (Amazon/KDP, ~320 words)

> Plain text with markdown bold. In KDP, apply the bold with the description editor's formatting (or KDP's supported HTML bold tag) when you paste it in. The first two lines are the above-the-fold hook (under 150 characters).

**They locked the Vampire Goddess away for three thousand years. An orphan with one hand just let her out.**

Bal Norheart was born with the Blood sygl, the one mark nobody wants. The doctors took his left hand before he could crawl. No sygl means no magic school, so Bal taught himself from a friend's secondhand spellbooks and stopped hoping for more.

Then the sealed temple behind his orphanage flickered.

Inside was Amelia: originator of the Blood, betrayed by the other gods, imprisoned for three millennia, and outraged to learn the world now calls her a myth. One drink of her blood regrows Bal's hand. One vow makes the goddess his familiar. Together they walk into the Heir's School of Magykal Artes, where Amelia takes the empty throne the gods kept for her and names Bal her High Priest in front of everyone.

Being the only Blood student in a school that fears you is not easy. Neither is the power that comes with Amelia's blood. Bal can change a person's sygl, but never against their will.

Viridia, the storm-haired heir of the god Raizen, refuses to play anyone's games. Lora, the shy rapier prodigy with an alchemy book always in her bag, would rather face a sword than a crowd. Both are falling for Bal. Raizen does not share what is his.

When the gods' war comes for the people Bal loves, he has to decide what kind of High Priest he will be.

***Sygl* is a warm, clean new adult academy fantasy.** It has:
- a magic school and a self-taught underdog mage
- rune-crafting, blood magic and a guild of adventurers
- a found family of misfits
- a slow-building harem romance with one hero and three heroines, all 18+, with the bedroom door firmly closed

If you've ever been the one nobody picked, this is the story of finding the people who choose you back, and a goddess who will take on every other god to keep them.

## 8. Comparable titles (5; all real, published books)

1. ***Cradle: Unsouled*, Will Wight (2016).** An outcast denied any path to power gets a second chance from an extraordinary patron and climbs from nothing. It's the closest match for Bal's arc and is already the book's planned comp. It's older than five years, but it defines the progression genre.
2. ***Mark of the Fool*, J.M. Clarke (Book 1, 2022).** A magic-academy progression fantasy whose hero carries the "worst" mark at the world's greatest school of wizardry. It's the same "branded outsider at the elite academy" draw, with a warm, comic slice-of-life tone.
3. ***A Deadly Education*, Naomi Novik (2020).** The heroine is shunned at a magic school because of her feared, "dark" affinity. It's the same hook as a Blood sygl student among the Heirs. Novik's book is darker and snarkier; *Sygl* is warmer.
4. ***Sufficiently Advanced Magic*, Andrew Rowe (2017).** An academy fantasy built on enchanting and magical craft. It's for readers who will enjoy Bal's rune work (the avoidance rune, the arrow-rune shirts, the purification rune under the cup).
5. ***Beware of Chicken*, Casualfarmer (2022).** A cozy, warm-hearted cultivation fantasy about choosing a peaceful life and a found family over the power game. It's a tone comp for *Sygl*'s low-angst warmth and its "break their toys" refusal to play the gods' game.

**Deliberately left out:**
- No clean *harem* comp is listed. I could not verify a currently selling, closed-door, one-hero/multiple-heroine academy title well enough to name it. Before launch, the author should browse the top 100 in the chosen categories and add one or two harem comps that are genuinely clean.
- *Fourth Wing* (Yarros) and *A Court of Thorns and Roses* (Maas) are left out despite the academy and romantasy overlap. Both carry an explicit-spice expectation that *Sygl* does not meet. ACOTAR is in STATE.yaml's original comp list; for this positioning, drop it.
