# Cover concepts — work in progress, nothing chosen yet

Front-cover exploration for the trilogy. **None of this is a deliverable** — the
shipping covers are still the ones under each book's `delivery/cover/`. Nothing
here is wired into `tools/compose_wrap.py`.

## Where it landed

`front-book-1.png` is the current direction and the author's pick: Viridia from
behind, walking through the open gates toward Hazel, photoreal art with the series
typography composited over it. Built by `../tools/compose_front_cover.py` from the
art bed in `art/`.

Books Two and Three have **no scene chosen yet**. The proposal is to repeat Book
One's device — her back to us, facing the thing she is walking into — so the three
read as a set: Book Two the resistance camp in the trees at dusk, Book Three the
source cavern from Book One Ch.14. Awaiting the author.

## The palette rule (why these are green and amber)

The first pass reused Scale & Silver's palette — the same white→silver→ember title
gradient and its moonlit blue. That was wrong: the trilogy is the **Post Peleos**
YA line and Scale & Silver is the **Søren Stromberg** adult line, and this whole
production round was about separating them. Sharing a palette would undo that on
the shelf.

So the trilogy owns **green-black and amber**, which the Stromberg cover does not
touch at all. Viridia — green, green eyes. Per book: One hazel-amber lamplight,
Two smoke and rust-red, Three pale violet void-light. Generated night art trends
blue/teal, so `compose_front_cover.py` warm-grades every bed toward green-amber.

## Brightness

Raw night art measured **15% mean luminance**, only 1.6% of pixels above 128, and
~85% ink coverage — too dark to survive a 160px retailer thumbnail and liable to
plug up on cream stock. The `lift()` step opens the shadows on a curve; the current
cover sits at **30% mean / 10.7% lit / ~70% ink**, which keeps the night mood with
detail intact.

## Resolution

`front-book-1.png` is 1000x1500 — a mockup, **below print resolution**. Re-render
the chosen one with `--print` (2452x3703). The type is vector so it re-renders
genuinely sharp; the Canva art bed still needs upscaling, same tradeoff recorded in
the per-book production notes.

## The earlier abstract concepts

`concept-{A,B,C}-*` are the first, rejected pass: flat vector emblems (keyhole,
fracture, eclipse) on black, generated by `../tools/cover_concepts.py`. Kept
because the generator is reusable and the arch layout in B may still be worth
something, but the author's call was that they read as minimalist graphics rather
than painted covers. `*-LINEUP.png` shows each concept as a three-book set.
