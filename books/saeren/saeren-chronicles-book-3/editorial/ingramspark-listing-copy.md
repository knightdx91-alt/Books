# IngramSpark listing copy — *The Weight of the Source*

The Saeren Chronicles, Book Three · by **Post Peleos** · upper-YA fantasy

Built from the back-cover copy printed on the r11 wrap, so the listing and the cover
agree. Ingram has **two separate description fields** and they are not interchangeable:
the long **Description** (~4,000 chars) that flows to Amazon and the rest, and the
**Short Description** (250 BYTES) for Ingram's catalogs.

> ⚠ The back-cover copy this is derived from was written by Claude and has **not yet
> had an author read**. Both are worth checking before they go live.

---

## DESCRIPTION field — plain text, paste as-is

```text
She put the world back together. Now she has to carry it.

Months after the severing was mended, the old order is ash. Every living soul carries both halves of the gift, and none of them asked for it. Viridia feels all of them at once — she is the source now, the still point a remade world turns on, and the one person who cannot put it down.

Then the portals begin to open.

The things that come through do not attack. They come toward her. Whatever is loose in the world is being pulled to the source, and the source is a girl who has already spent almost everything she had holding it together.

In saving her world, she made the thing that will unmake it.

What she does with that power — and what she refuses to become in order to use it — is the last question The Saeren Chronicles will ask of her.

The conclusion of the trilogy: a fantasy about power held without cruelty, the people who keep you human, and the weight of being what everyone else needs.

Upper-YA fantasy. Book Three of The Saeren Chronicles, following Hazel Academy and The Resistance.
```

Deliberately not hard-wrapped, so it pastes into the form as clean paragraphs.

**Spoiler line held:** it states the situation Book Two ends in — unavoidable for a
Book Three blurb, and standard — but names nothing about who survives, what the cost
of the source finally is, or how it ends.

**"Following Hazel Academy and The Resistance" is doing real work.** It is the only
line telling a browser this is Book Three, and readers abandon a series page fast when
they cannot tell the reading order.

## SHORT DESCRIPTION field — 250 BYTE cap, pure ASCII

The cap is in **bytes**, not characters — an em dash costs 3 — so this is plain ASCII
and the count is what it looks like.

```text
Viridia mended a broken world and became its source. Now the portals are opening, and the things coming through are not attacking. They are being pulled toward her. The conclusion of The Saeren Chronicles. Upper-YA fantasy.
```
